## How the price is set
The bank first purchases the asset the customer needs and takes ownership of it, together with the risks of ownership, before selling it. The bank then sells the asset to the customer at its cost plus a disclosed profit margin. The sale price is fixed when the contract is signed and does not change if market rates move during the finance term.

## Difference from interest
Interest is a charge on money lent over time. Murabaha profit is part of the price of a real asset that the bank owned before the sale. The customer's total payable amount is known in advance.

## Early settlement
Customers may settle early at any time. The bank may grant a rebate on the unearned portion of the profit. The rebate is granted at the bank's discretion and is not a contractual right.

## Late payment
If an instalment is overdue, the customer may be asked to pay a late payment amount that is donated to charity. It is not income for the bank and it does not compound. Repeated late payment may be reported to the credit bureau.
