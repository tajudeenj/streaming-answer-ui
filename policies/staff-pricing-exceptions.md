## Exception authority
Profit-margin exceptions below the published rate require approval from the regional credit head. Exceptions above 50 basis points require the chief risk officer's approval.

## Documentation
Every exception must record the customer segment, competing offer evidence and the approving officer. Exceptions are reviewed monthly by the pricing committee.
