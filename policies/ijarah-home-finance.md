## Ownership
In Ijarah home finance the bank owns the property and leases it to the customer for an agreed rent. Ownership transfers to the customer at the end of the term once all rent has been paid.

## Maintenance and damage
The bank, as owner, is responsible for major structural maintenance and for insuring the property through Takaful. The customer is responsible for routine upkeep and for any damage caused by misuse or negligence.

## Total loss
If the property is destroyed and can no longer be used, rent stops from the date of the loss, because rent cannot be charged for an asset that cannot be used. Takaful proceeds are applied according to the lease.

## Rent review
Rent may be reviewed on the dates stated in the lease. Any review follows the benchmark and cap written in the contract, and the customer is notified in advance.
