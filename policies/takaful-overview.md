## What Takaful is
Takaful is cooperative protection. Participants contribute to a shared fund that is used to help any participant who suffers a covered loss. The operator manages the fund for a disclosed fee.

## Surplus
If the fund has a surplus at the end of the period, part of it may be distributed to participants according to the policy terms.

## Use with finance
Ijarah and Murabaha finance may require Takaful cover on the financed asset. The required cover is stated in the finance offer.
