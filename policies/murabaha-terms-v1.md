## How the price is set
The bank purchases the asset and sells it to the customer at cost plus a disclosed profit. The sale price is fixed at signing.

## Early settlement
Early settlement rebates are not offered under this version of the terms. The full sale price remains payable.

## Late payment
A late payment charge applies to overdue instalments. The amount is donated to charity and is not income for the bank.
